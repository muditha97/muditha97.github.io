01 — ABOUT

From requirements to production, and what happens after.

I've spent 5+ years building production Node.js and NestJS services on AWS, and today I lead the booking and integrations domain of a UK hospitality SaaS platform. I work across the stack, with web and mobile frontends in React and React Native. Much of my work sits where systems meet — third-party integrations, event-driven pipelines, and the retries, idempotency and recovery paths that keep them consistent when things fail.

I work across the full lifecycle: understanding the business requirement, designing the system, implementing the feature, writing tests for the rules that matter, integrating services, and improving operational reliability once it's live.

Outside my day job I build SaaS products independently — NutriPlatform is live — and I'm specialising in applied AI engineering: retrieval-augmented generation, local models, and agentic workflows that make engineering teams faster without taking humans out of the decisions.
